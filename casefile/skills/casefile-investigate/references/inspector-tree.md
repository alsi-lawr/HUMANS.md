# Inspector-tree investigation

Require the selected `casefile-investigate-inspector-tree` matrix, nested workers, and shared
writable planning storage. Give each inspector a disjoint domain and authorise bounded
decomposition. Detectives report candidates; inspectors verify evidence and recommend disposition.
The root retains cross-domain duplicate authority, ticket-path reservation, and final disposition.

For Codex, the root runs the delegation check in `casefile` for each inspector. Give the inspector
the exact strategy target and scope for its authorized detectives. The inspector repeats the check
from its own session before every detective assignment and reports unavailable launches to the root.
