# Casefile Investigate

Load `casefile` and the strategy matrix. Present the compatible investigation strategies, recommend one from scope and capabilities, and wait for explicit selection:

- [Solo](references/solo.md) for narrow inseparable work.
- [Atomic](references/atomic.md) for disjoint parallel questions.
- [Inspector tree](references/inspector-tree.md) for bounded hierarchical decomposition.

Investigators report candidates before ticket creation. The root verifies evidence, arbitrates uniqueness, reserves IDs and paths, and records accepted or rejected disposition.
