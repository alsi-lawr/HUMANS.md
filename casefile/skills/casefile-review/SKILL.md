# Casefile Review

Load `casefile`, validate the ticket set, and present compatible review strategies with a recommendation. Wait for explicit selection:

- [Atomic](references/atomic.md) for independent disjoint groups.
- [Dialogue](references/dialogue.md) for chair-and-challenger reconciliation.
- [Two stage](references/two-stage.md) for primary review plus focused verification.

Reviewers write evidence only. The root reconciles duplicates and decisions, routes corrections to the original owner, and escalates unresolved contention to the human.
