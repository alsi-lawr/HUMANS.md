# Casefile Review: Dialogue

Validate the selected matrix and nesting support. Spawn one chair; the chair spawns the authorised challenger. Both inspect independently, then conduct at most two focused reconciliation rounds. The chair records the joint verdict, agreed corrections, unresolved contentions, evidence, and ticket IDs. The root routes correction and escalation; reviewers edit neither source nor tickets.
