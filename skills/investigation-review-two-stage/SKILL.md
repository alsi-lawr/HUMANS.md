# Investigation Review: Two Stage

Validate and persist the selected matrix. Assign the primary reviewer the complete ticket set or selected batch. Give its concrete findings and acceptance result to a verification reviewer, who confirms or rejects those points without repeating the whole review. Store both reports, then let the root reconcile, route corrections, and escalate contention.
