# Investigation: Solo

Load the umbrella ticketed investigation skill. Require and persist a selected compatible matrix even though it has no workers. The root investigates read-only, performs duplicate arbitration, reserves IDs, authors provisional tickets, verifies evidence, and disposes them. No subagents are spawned.
