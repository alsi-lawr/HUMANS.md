# Casefile Review: Two Stage

Validate and persist the selected matrix. Give the primary reviewer the complete ticket set or bounded batch. Give its concrete findings and acceptance result to a verifier, who confirms or rejects those points without repeating the review. Store both reports; the root reconciles, routes corrections, and escalates contention.
